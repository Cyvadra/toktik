# Slogan

让期权投资变得简单

# What kind of users do we target at?

- Options trading beginner
- Experienced options trader
- Options quantitative developer

# What will our platform provide?

根据宏观市场环境、品种期权趋势&波动、品种期权曲面特征，等多种实际情况因素，整理出市场环境和期权特征上下文，

使用AI Agent之类的计算方法，进行计算/匹配，评估合适的期权交易策略，推荐给用户。

# What do I have?

- Option chain market data.
- Classic and preset options trading strategies (those in references).
- Network access. (for stock/crypto market news and other public data)
- Other data if you ask me to collect.

- US Magnificent 7: AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, NVDA, META, TSLA
- Crypto: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL
- 其他全球核心品种

# What do we demonstrate to users?

- Recommendations for trading options:

  - 在每个特定期权上的特定交易策略：评分、胜率、盈亏比等，收益和风险评估

- Recommendations for selling options:

  - 期权卖方品种和策略推荐

  - Options you suggested , should at least have 3/4 advantage, on [Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta].
